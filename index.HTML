Your Questions Answered

How do I apply for undergraduate courses?

+

All full-time undergraduate applications must be made via UCAS. Use the code L24 for Liverpool Hope University.

Is on-campus accommodation guaranteed?

+

We guarantee campus housing for all first-year undergraduate students who firm their offer by the UCAS deadline.

What scholarships are available for 2026?

+

From the Access to Hope scholarship to International Merit awards, we offer over £1M in annual student support.
